Match The Stack

Tap visible cards from the layered board and collect them in the seven-slot tray. Three cards with the same rank disappear. Clear the whole board before the tray fills up.

Poker cards are used as puzzle pieces only. There is no betting, casino play, real-money gambling, account system, or user-generated content. Ads may appear on result screens after later levels.
