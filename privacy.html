Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 5, 2026

Overview

Poker Stack Match is a standalone puzzle game. It does not require an account and does not include purchases, betting, real-money gambling, or user-generated content. The app includes advertising and security SDKs used to request ad placement configuration, load ads, and help protect the app.

Data Collection

The app may request App Tracking Transparency permission. If you authorize tracking, advertising partners may use the device advertising identifier to deliver and measure ads. If you do not authorize tracking, the app may still show ads, but they should not use the advertising identifier for tracking.

The app itself does not require a user account and does not ask you to provide a name, email address, phone number, payment information, or profile information.

Local Device Storage

The app may store gameplay progress and settings on your device, including completed levels, selected level progress, music preferences, and sound preferences. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us or to third parties.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly ask children or any other users to provide personal information. Advertising partners may process limited device and ad-delivery data as described in their own privacy practices.

Network Use And Ads

The app may connect to backend services to request ad placement configuration and may load ads from third-party advertising platforms. Ads are shown on result screens after level 3. Gameplay progress and settings are stored locally.

Third-Party Services

The app may integrate third-party advertising SDKs, including ad platforms such as Chartboost, InMobi, Vungle, and Yandex Mobile Ads. These partners may process device, ad-request, impression, and interaction data according to their own privacy policies.

Changes To This Policy

If the app's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated before the change is released. The effective date at the top of this page will show the latest update date.

Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the support contact provided on the App Store product page for Poker Stack Match.
